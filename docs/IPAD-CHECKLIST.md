# iPad checklist (manual, before merging anything to `main`)

Automated tests cannot prove the iPad works (WebKit/iPad is not covered unless `touch` in `npm test` did not say SKIPPED). Do this on the real iPad with the app opened from the home-screen icon, and write the date and result in the pull request.

1. Open the app from the home-screen icon (not from Safari).
2. Complete one lesson with its quiz, then one round of questions of each kind: multiple choice, typed answer, clock, put in order, colour in fractions.
3. Tap **Next** quickly several times in a row: it moves one step per tap, with no skipped steps and no dead taps.
4. Close the app completely (swipe it away), reopen it: progress is still there (stars, medals, stickers).
5. Grown-ups area → **Exportar**: does a file download and can you open it from Files? If not, use **Compartir copia** and choose *Save to Files*. Write down which one works.
6. Grown-ups area → **Compartir copia**: the share sheet opens and the file can be saved.
7. Airplane mode: the app still opens and works (today this is NOT VERIFIED).
8. Rotate the iPad both ways: no picture breaks.
9. Make a backup now (step 5 or 6) and keep it: it is the safety net for every later change.
10. **Today's mission** (hub): play it through; the stage screens, the lesson inside it and the end screen all respond to taps.
11. **Me**: change hair, top and hat; try on something you cannot afford (it only previews, nothing is charged); buy something with ⭐ and see the balance drop. Close and reopen: the outfit is still worn.
12. **Friends**: open a sticker pack; a new friend appears the first time. The Friends screen shows the pictures sharply (not blurry) and the hidden ones as ???.
